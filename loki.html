🐉 Nidhogg — use it right
• Melee-immune & flying — park it over melee-heavy armies for free, untouchable damage.
• Hunt villagers — its best target is an undefended economy; workers can't shoot back.
• It forces archers — when they build Toxotes to answer it, your Boars/Hersirs eat those archers. Win either way.
• Cast on commitment — fly it just ahead of the giant deathball; pull it out if dedicated anti-air is up, never suicide it.
