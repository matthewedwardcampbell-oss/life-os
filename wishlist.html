Rule of thumb the app applies: durable goods → wait for the sale window · consumables → buy to max at best $/unit when you hit min.
How the min/max help works. You don't have to guess the numbers. For anything you buy repeatedly, the app watches how fast you go through it and proposes a min (reorder point — hit this, it nudges you) and a max (how much to stock up to). For one-off durable goods it sets min/max to 1 so they just sit on the wish list until bought.

Compare handles two cases with the same card you already know from the Recipe Lab: different brands of the same thing (Cuisinart vs Ninja) and different versions of the same product (high vs low density roller) — pros, cons, and price side by side, with one marked “for you” based on how you'll use it.

Sale radar + the 🔔 watch line tell you whether to pounce or hold: it knows the big sale windows coming up and roughly what each item tends to drop to, so a $27 roller today waits 32 days for a likely $19.
