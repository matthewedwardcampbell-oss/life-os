🧬 AI Model League — primer

Your idea, mapped to the real research around it. Learning path (ordered, not alphabetical):

1
Model merging — most intuitive; run it first with mergekit.
2
Population-Based Training — parallel competition, winners propagate.
3
Evolutionary Model Merge — where 1 + 2 combine = your idea (Sakana AI, 2024).

◆ The novel lever: merge the losers, don't kill them — testable, falsifiable, under-explored.

● DATA TITHE ●
tithe_id:   ai.model-league.primer.v1
domain:     artificial-intelligence / machine-learning
purpose:    learning-primer + ingestion seed for a channel
origin:     "AI Model League" (competitive parallel build + merge)
maturity:   [ established | emerging | frontier | speculative ]

CONCEPTS
- population_based_training  [established] DeepMind 2017
    weak models copy winners' weights+settings, then mutate.
    -> "teams that win get more each round"
- league_training            [established] AlphaStar 2019
    champions vs exploiter rivals -> arms race, no collapse.
- model_merging              [emerging]   soups/SLERP/TIES/DARE
    blend model WEIGHTS; result inherits both skillsets.
    ! must share a base model (same architecture).
    -> "smaller teams merge, maybe an upgrade"
- evolutionary_model_merge   [frontier]   Sakana AI 2024
    evolve WHICH models to merge + HOW -> new abilities.
    -> "could yield a different AI"  (= your whole idea)
- mixture_of_experts         [established]
    many specialists + a router picks per input.
- automl / nas               [established]
    AI systems that design/tune other AI models.
- neuroevolution (NEAT)      [established] Stanley 2002
    evolve nets: mutate, cross-breed, select fittest.

HYPOTHESIS (the novel lever)
  standard: evolution KILLS losers.
  yours:    MERGE losers (and winners) instead.
  tension:  preserve diversity + improve  ...OR... collapse to mediocre average?
  status:   open / testable / under-explored

EXPERIMENT (weekend-scale)
  tool:   mergekit + Hugging Face
  1  pick 4-6 models sharing ONE base (e.g. Mistral-7B fine-tunes)
  2  score each on a benchmark      = the tournament
  3a merge the BOTTOM teams         = your hypothesis
  3b merge the TOP teams            = control
  4  re-score the offspring
  win:  merged underdogs BEAT their parents
  kill: merges regress to the mean
  wall: compute (GPU hours), not ideas

GLOSSARY
  weights      the learned numbers = a model's knowledge
  fine-tune    a base model trained further on a narrow task
  base_model   the shared ancestor; required for clean merging
  benchmark    a fixed test set to score/compare models
  emergent     a capability of the whole, absent in the parts
● END TITHE ●

🧪 Why "falsifiable" matters

A claim is falsifiable if a specific result could prove it wrong (Popper's line between science and faith). The tithe's kill criterion — "merges regress to the mean" — is what makes the hypothesis real instead of just hopeful. If nothing could ever disprove it, it teaches you nothing.
