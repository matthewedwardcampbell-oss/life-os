🔔 Follow-ups
the daily outreach queue

🎯 The rule

Your morning CRM block has one job: send a handful of warm, specific messages. Rebook targets first, win-backs second, new leads same-day.

📅 Rebook cadence

Repeat clients: a light check-in ~3–4 weeks after their last session, before they drift. Lapsed: a no-pressure note at ~8–10 weeks.

✎ Once the export's in, this becomes a dated list: who's due this week, pulled from last-seen.

💬 Rebook text — steal this

"Hey [name] — it's Matthew with Stone Therapy. You're about due for a reset on that [shoulder / low back]. I've got a couple mornings open this week if you want me to come to you. Want me to hold one?"
Win-back: "Hey [name], been a minute! I'm back in the Denver area full-time and booking mobile sessions again. Want to get you back on the table?"

🏥 Insurance / HSA
covered care = higher ticket

⚡ Fastest win · HSA / FSA

Massage is HSA/FSA-eligible with a Letter of Medical Necessity from a doctor. Their card runs like a normal Visa through your processor — you just hand them a superbill. Many current clients have an HSA and don't know they can use it on you.

low lift · do first

🧾 Setup checklist

1
Get an NPI — free at nppes.cms.gov. Nothing bills without it.
2
Accept HSA/FSA cards — usually already works through Square/Stripe.
3
Superbill template — your info, date, CPT (97124 massage), amount.
4
Pick a lane — cash + superbill (client submits) vs. auto-injury / workers-comp.

📣 Get in front
how to open the door

🪑 Door-opener · free chair demo

Offer a clinic 15 minutes of free chair massage for their staff. The front desk and providers become fans — and fans refer. Nothing sells you like hands-on.

🥪 Lunch-and-learn

Bring lunch, give a 10-min talk on how massage improves their patients' outcomes (recovery, pain, range of motion). You leave as the name they remember.

📄 One-pager + cards

A clean leave-behind: what you do, that you're mobile, that you take HSA/FSA and injury referrals, and an easy booking link. Drop it in person, not by email.

🔁 Make referring easy

Hand them referral cards or a QR to your booking page, and refer back — send your clients to them too. Reciprocity makes it stick. Thank them every time they send someone.

👣 This week

Walk into 3 chiropractors near your route, offer the free staff chair demo, leave the one-pager. That's the whole play to start.
