🧭 Game flow
⛺
1 · Settle
Drop the town center near fresh water + forest + fertile land. First workers to food & wood.
❄️
2 · Beat winter
Before the first winter: enough food stockpile + firewood/coats. This is the make-or-break early wall.
⚙️
3 · Chains
Stand up first production chain (e.g. wheat→flour→bread) to raise happiness.
⚖️
4 · Trade
Turn surplus into coin at the market; buy what your land can't make.
🗺️
5 · Expand & era up
Claim territory for new resources; push research to the next era.
