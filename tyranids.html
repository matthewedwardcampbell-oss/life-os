🧭 Battle flow
🌫️
1 · Hide
Start stealthed in an asteroid/nebula field (no asteroid damage to you). Let them cross the map.
🎯
2 · Pick target
Mark their command ship or an isolated straggler. Don't commit the whole swarm into their line.
🚀
3 · Lunge
Pop the speed-boost to close fast; stagger ships so you don't pile up.
🐙
4 · Latch & board
Latch the tentacle to pin + DoT, then dump boarding while acid melts hull.
🦷
5 · Devour → next
Break it, peel off, repeat. If you're getting kited or shelled, disengage — don't chase.
